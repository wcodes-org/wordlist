# Greylist

Current dictionary words that may be replaced, or whose emoji need work, in a later version. They stay in the live list until a new version is issued.

This is not a decision that every word below will be replaced. Some items are emoji issues only.

### Violence, danger or negative

- `army` — military / violence
- `blood` — violence
- `fire` — danger; shares 🔥 with `flame`, `oven`, `plasma`, `stove`
- `flame` — danger; shares 🔥 with `fire`
- `gas` — danger; also ambiguous (fuel vs state of matter)
- `plutonium` — weapons-adjacent
- `powder` — ambiguous (including explosive)
- `punch` — violence
- `risk` — negative
- `smoke` — danger
- `soldier` — military / violence
- `spy` — crime
- `syringe` — disease / medical
- `tank` — military / weapon
- `tremor` — disaster / medical
- `uranium` — weapons-adjacent

### More than one pronunciation

- `bow` — bow (weapon) vs bow (bend); also a weapon association
- `live` — verb vs adjective (short vs long "i")

### Homophones or confusable spelling

- `angle` — confusable with "angel"
- `plane` — confusable with "plain"
- `week` — confusable with "weak"

### Irregular spelling

- `receipt` — silent "p"

### Unfamiliar or regional

- `petrol` — regional (gasoline in some varieties of English)
- `quince` — less familiar than fruit such as mango
- `sleet` — less familiar in much of India

### Ambiguous or abstract

- `chamber` — many senses, including a gun chamber
- `free` — abstract, not a concrete noun
- `mask` — several senses (face covering, theatre, verb)
- `name` — abstract
- `news` — abstract / mass noun
- `noise` — abstract
- `nut` — several senses (food, hardware, slang)
- `place` — abstract / generic
- `pleasure` — abstract
- `system` — abstract / generic
- `touch` — a sense / verb as much as a noun
- `user` — generic / technical, not a concrete object
- `verse` — abstract; also close to "versus"

### Cardinal directions — easy to mix up when heard

- `east`
- `north`
- `south`
- `west` — especially east / west over a poor line

### Places with ambiguous names

- `hall` — sounds like “haul”
- `mall` — sounds like “maul”
- `station` — can mean a railway stop, a broadcaster or a work area

- `equator` — not a concrete object
- `horn` — several senses (animal, vehicle, instrument)
- `quilt` — soft item; shares 🛏️ with `bed`, `chamber`
- `quiver` — less familiar; shares 🏹 with `bow`
- `rake` — also a verb; shares 🧹 with `broom`, `mop`
- `rent` — abstract / financial, not a thing
- `rim` — weak / vague
- `road` — generic place; close in use to `street`
- `rub` — weak as a spoken noun; also a verb
- `rust` — decay / negative
- `school` — generic institution
- `shake` — verb-like, many senses
- `shout` — verb-like, not a strong noun
- `sink` — noun / verb ambiguity
- `smell` — a sense, not a tangible object
- `street` — generic place; close in use to `road`
- `tape` — many senses
- `town` — generic place; overlaps `village` / city
- `turnip` — easy to mix with other root vegetables; shares 🥕 with `carrot`, `radish`
- `veil` — several senses; less everyday
- `video` — generic / dated as an object name
- `village` — generic place; overlaps `town`
