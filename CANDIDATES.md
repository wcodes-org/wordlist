# Words under review

Current dictionary words that may be replaced, or whose emoji need work, in a later version. They stay in the live list until a new version is issued. See [RULES.md](RULES.md).

This is not a decision that every word below will be replaced. Some items are emoji issues only.

### Violence, danger or negative (rules 9, 20)

- `army` — military / violence
- `blood` — violence
- `fire` — danger; shares 🔥 with `flame`, `oven`, `plasma`, `stove`
- `flame` — danger; shares 🔥 with `fire`
- `gas` — danger; also ambiguous (fuel vs state of matter)
- `plutonium` — weapons-adjacent
- `powder` — ambiguous (including explosive)
- `punch` — violence
- `risk` — negative
- `smoke` — danger
- `soldier` — military / violence
- `spy` — crime
- `syringe` — disease / medical
- `tank` — military / weapon
- `tremor` — disaster / medical
- `uranium` — weapons-adjacent

### Brand names (rule 20)

- `jeep` — a brand name

### More than one pronunciation (rule 16)

- `bow` — bow (weapon) vs bow (bend); also a weapon association
- `live` — verb vs adjective (short vs long "i")

### Homophones or confusable spelling (rules 6, 11)

- `angle` — confusable with "angel"
- `plane` — confusable with "plain"
- `week` — confusable with "weak"

### Irregular spelling (rule 17)

- `receipt` — silent "p"

### Unfamiliar or regional (rules 4, 18)

- `petrol` — regional (gasoline in some varieties of English)
- `quince` — called out in rule 18 as less familiar than e.g. mango
- `sleet` — less familiar in much of India

### Ambiguous or abstract (rules 1, 2)

- `chamber` — many senses, including a gun chamber
- `free` — abstract, not a concrete noun
- `mask` — several senses (face covering, theatre, verb)
- `name` — abstract
- `news` — abstract / mass noun
- `noise` — abstract
- `nut` — several senses (food, hardware, slang)
- `place` — abstract / generic
- `pleasure` — abstract
- `system` — abstract / generic
- `touch` — a sense / verb as much as a noun
- `user` — generic / technical, not a concrete object
- `verse` — abstract; also close to "versus"

### Cardinal directions — easy to mix up when heard

- `east`
- `north`
- `south`
- `west` — especially east / west over a poor line

### Emoji: ZWJ sequences (emoji rules)

These words can stay; the emoji should be replaced with a single code point (plus VS16), not a ZWJ sequence.

- `cook` — 👨‍🍳
- `doctor` — 👨‍⚕️
- `farmer` — 👨‍🌾
- `nurse` — 👩‍⚕️
- `pilot` — 👨‍✈️
- `student` — 🧑‍🎓
- `teacher` — 👨‍🏫

### Emoji: shared by more than one word (emoji rules)

The emoji rules ask for one unique emoji per word. Many words still share an emoji; these 12 are the most confusing cases, where the picture is also another word on the list. A full emoji pass is still needed.

- `sunrise`, `dawn`, `morning` — all 🌅
- `puzzle`, `part` — both 🧩
- `fire`, `flame` — both 🔥 (also used by `oven`, `plasma`, `stove`)
- `bow`, `quiver` — both 🏹
- `plane`, `jet`, `trip` — all ✈️
